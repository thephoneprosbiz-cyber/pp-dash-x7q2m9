Sync across devices (Google)

Not connected.
Keeps this dashboard's data the same on every device where you're signed in and synced (uses a private file in your own Google Drive - not visible in your normal Drive folder). Whichever device has the newer changes wins when you sync, so sync right before switching devices to avoid losing edits. Same connection as the Google Calendar sync on the Pickup Calendar tab - you only need to connect once.

Data and backups

Your data is saved automatically in this browser only. Export a backup regularly so you never lose it - especially before clearing browser data or switching computers.
